# Credit Sense Model Card

## Status
**Prototype / synthetic data only.** No production lending use.

## Intended use
Engineering prototype for evaluating an alternative-data decisioning workflow for U.S. community lenders serving thin-file/near-prime borrowers. It is not a creditworthiness determination and must not be deployed without independent validation and legal/compliance review.

## Data
20,000 synthetic applicants with temporal application dates. Traditional repayment/credit variables and synthetic permissioned utility/rent and cash-flow variables. Protected attributes are generated solely for fairness auditing and excluded from model features.

## Models
1. Rule-based baseline.
2. Logistic regression, traditional variables.
3. Monotonic HistGradientBoosting, traditional variables.
4. Monotonic HistGradientBoosting, traditional + alternative variables, isotonic calibration.

## Metrics
See `outputs/metrics.json`. These are holdout results on synthetic data and should not be interpreted as real-world validation.

## Fairness
See `outputs/fairness_report.md`. Selection-rate disparities, adverse-impact ratios, group error/calibration summaries, and proxy correlations are exploratory synthetic diagnostics.

## Explainability
SHAP top factors are mapped to reason codes. The mapping is feature-driven; reasons are not generated by an LLM. Reason-code wording is an ECOA/Reg B-style prototype convention, not legal advice or a determination that the notice is legally sufficient.

## Human review
Applicants near the PD cutoff or with low confidence are routed to manual review. Confidence is a heuristic, not a probability of correctness.

## Monitoring and revalidation triggers
Revalidate before any material population, product, data-source, policy, or feature change; after observed drift; after fairness deterioration; after material calibration degradation; after adverse-action complaint patterns; and at scheduled model-risk review intervals.

## Known limitations
Synthetic label-generation process may create unrealistic relationships. No real bureau, transaction, lender, or repayment data are present. No causal claims can be made. Fairness results depend on synthetic group construction. Real-world legal obligations, consent, FCRA/ECOA/Reg B requirements, adverse-action notice requirements, data-provider contracts, and model governance are not established by this prototype.
